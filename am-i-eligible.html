Before you apply

Am I eligible? An honest answer

Rather than make you guess, here is the plain version of who we can house today.

Joseph’s Home · Saucier, Mississippi · 2 min read · updated August 2026

“A bruised reed he will not break, and a faintly burning wick he will not quench.”

Isaiah 42:3 · ESV

You are likely eligible if

  • You are a man, eighteen or older.
  • You have completed a rehab program, or will have before you arrive.
  • You are willing to live under the house rules and be discipled.
  • You are able and willing to work.
  • You will attend church and church activities. This is not optional here.

Things that are not automatic disqualifiers

A criminal record. Probation. Prescribed medication. Debt. A bad discharge from a previous program. No church background. Not being sure what you believe. Having failed before — including having failed here.

We would rather have a man who tells us about all of it up front than a man with a clean-looking application and a surprise in it.

Where we are not the right place

If you are still using or need to detox. We are not a rehab and we cannot supervise a withdrawal. Withdrawal from alcohol and from some prescription medications can be medically dangerous and needs a doctor, not a house of men praying over you. Finish treatment first. Come to us after.

If you need medical or psychiatric care we cannot provide. We are a home, not a clinic. Some men need a level of care that is above what a sober living house can honestly offer, and pretending otherwise would not be kindness.

If you are a woman. Today we house men. We have prayed for years about a home for women, and it is not here yet. If you are a woman reading this, please still write to us — we know the coast and we will point you somewhere real.

If you are in danger right now, call 911. For free and confidential help any hour of the day, call or text 988. For treatment referrals near you, SAMHSA’s national helpline is 1-800-662-4357, also free and confidential.

If the answer today is no

It is not a verdict on you. Rehab first, then us, is a sequence, not a rejection. Call us anyway. We would rather spend twenty minutes helping you find the right door than have you decide there isn’t one.
