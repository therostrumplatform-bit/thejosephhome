A faith-based sober living home

A man is not his worst year.

Joseph's Home is a faith-based sober living home in Saucier, Mississippi for men who have finished rehab and are ready to break the cycle for good — through discipleship, work, accountability, and enduring brotherhood.

Our mission

We exist to disciple men to become who God created them to be.

Joseph's Home walks with men through recovery, discipleship, work, and the daily disciplines of rebuilding a life.

The Three-Year Walk

Ninety days was never going to do it.

Most men arrive having spent years building the thing they are trying to leave. Thirty days interrupts it. Three years replaces it.

Phase 1 · Transition12 months in-house

Built on the RESTORE journal: a bed, schedule, chores, work, church and accountability.

Phase 2 · Mentorship24 months

Built on the Supporting Restoration journal.
